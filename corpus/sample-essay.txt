The Lighthouse of Alexandria

The Lighthouse of Alexandria, also called the Pharos, was one of the Seven Wonders of the
Ancient World. It was built on the island of Pharos in the harbour of Alexandria, Egypt,
during the reign of Ptolemy II, and completed around 280 BC.

The tower was said to be between 100 and 130 metres tall, which made it one of the tallest
man-made structures on Earth for many centuries. Its light, produced by a furnace and
polished bronze mirrors, could supposedly be seen dozens of kilometres away.

The lighthouse was badly damaged by earthquakes between 956 and 1323 AD and eventually
collapsed. Its stones were later used to build the Citadel of Qaitbay on the same site.
